Ai revenue recovery
Razorpay already has recovery agents; METIS becomes the counterfactual intelligence 
layer that tells those agents when an intervention will actually create incremental revenue, 
how much it is worth, and which action maximizes net recovery.

Whats the problem?
Payment fails, we dont know what action makes the customer pay again.

Our model
What would happen if we did nothing, and how much better would each possible intervention make things?

Razor pay agents can:-detect failed revenue → choose a recovery action → execute it.
What we are adding:-How do we avoid wasting recovery actions and spend our effort only where it will actually create incremental revenue?

Here are the final functional and non-functional requirements for METIS.


Functional Requirements
FR1 — Payment & Customer Data Ingestion
The system shall ingest payment, customer, merchant, and previous recovery-interaction data through APIs/webhooks or uploaded datasets.
FR2 — At-Risk Payment Detection
The system shall identify failed, overdue, or otherwise recoverable payments and calculate revenue at risk.
FR3 — Customer Recovery Propensity
The system shall estimate the probability that a customer will pay without any intervention.
FR4 — Intervention Outcome Prediction
The system shall estimate the expected recovery outcome for each permitted intervention:
Retry
Reminder
Payment link
Payment plan
Negotiation
Escalation
FR5 — Counterfactual/Uplift Estimation
The system shall estimate the incremental impact of each intervention compared with doing nothing.
FR6 — Recovery Value Optimization
The system shall calculate the expected incremental net revenue for each intervention after considering:
Recovery amount
Concession/discount
Intervention cost
Customer-contact/fatigue cost
FR7 — Best-Action Selection
The system shall select the intervention with the highest expected incremental net value while respecting merchant policies.
FR8 — Policy & Guardrail Enforcement
The system shall enforce configurable constraints such as:
Maximum discount
Maximum contacts
Maximum negotiation attempts
Minimum payable amount
Allowed recovery channels
Stopping conditions
FR9 — AI Recovery Agent
The system shall use an AI agent to communicate with customers, understand responses, negotiate within permitted limits, and execute the selected recovery workflow.
FR10 — Razorpay Integration
The system shall integrate with Razorpay APIs/MCP for supported actions such as payment links and payment-status verification.
FR11 — Webhook Processing
The system shall process payment-status webhooks, verify events, handle duplicate events, and update recovery outcomes.
FR12 — Human Escalation
The system shall escalate cases to a human when confidence is low, policies are exceeded, or automated recovery is inappropriate.
FR13 — Audit Trail
The system shall record:
Model decision
Predicted outcomes
Selected intervention
Policy checks
Agent actions
Customer responses
Payment outcome
FR14 — Experimentation & Measurement
The system shall compare different recovery strategies using control/treatment groups and calculate:
Recovery rate
Incremental lift
Incremental revenue
Net recovery
Intervention cost
FR15 — Explainability
The system shall explain why an intervention was selected, including the predicted counterfactual and expected incremental value.
FR16 — Recovery Dashboard
The system shall provide a dashboard showing:
Revenue at risk
Expected natural recovery
Recommended intervention
Expected incremental revenue
Actual recovery
Experiment results
Agent/audit activity
Non-Functional Requirements
NFR1 — Reliability
Payment and recovery workflows shall be fault-tolerant and safely recover from webhook duplication, API failures, agent failures, and interrupted workflows.
NFR2 — Idempotency
Financial actions shall be idempotent so duplicate events or retries cannot accidentally create duplicate payment actions.
NFR3 — Security
All sensitive customer/payment information shall be encrypted in transit and at rest, with secure authentication and authorization.
NFR4 — Financial Safety
The LLM shall never have unrestricted authority over financial actions. Every financial action must pass deterministic policy/guardrail validation.
NFR5 — Performance
Normal recovery decisions should be generated within ~1–2 seconds, excluding external payment-provider/network latency.
NFR6 — Scalability
The architecture shall support scaling from thousands to millions of payment/recovery events without redesigning the core decision pipeline.
NFR7 — Availability
The system should target 99.9% availability for the core decision and recovery services.
NFR8 — Observability
The system shall provide structured logs, metrics, traces, model-decision monitoring, and recovery-outcome monitoring.
NFR9 — Model Quality
Models shall be evaluated on held-out data using appropriate metrics such as:
AUCPR
calibration
uplift/Qini-related metrics
recovery lift
false-positive/negative impact
NFR10 — Explainability & Auditability
Every automated financial recommendation shall be traceable to the inputs, model output, policy decision, and final action.
NFR11 — Privacy
The system shall minimize stored customer data and enforce appropriate retention/deletion policies.
NFR12 — Maintainability
The system shall use modular components so ML models, policies, agents, and payment integrations can be updated independently.
NFR13 — Extensibility
New interventions, merchants, payment methods, recovery channels, and ML models shall be addable without changing the entire system.
NFR14 — Safety Under Uncertainty
When model confidence is below a configurable threshold, the system shall prefer no action or human escalation rather than an aggressive recovery action.
NFR15 — Regulatory/Business Compliance
Recovery communications and actions shall respect merchant-defined policies, applicable payment rules, consent requirements, and communication limits.
Features 
1. Revenue-at-Risk Detection
Automatically identify failed, overdue, or at-risk payments and calculate the total revenue currently at risk.
2. Customer Recovery Profile
Build a dynamic recovery profile using payment history, failure reasons, customer behavior, previous recovery attempts, payment preferences, and engagement history.
3. Natural Payment Propensity
Predict the probability that a customer will pay without any intervention.

Predict the expected outcome of different recovery actions:
Retry payment
Payment reminder
Payment link
Alternate payment method
Payment plan
Negotiation
Human escalation
5. Counterfactual Recovery Engine
Estimate what would happen with and without each intervention and calculate the incremental impact of every action.
6. Incremental Revenue Optimizer
Calculate the expected incremental net revenue of each intervention after accounting for recovery value, concessions, intervention cost, and customer-contact fatigue.
7. Intelligent Action Selection
Automatically select the highest-value recovery strategy for each customer.
The system can also decide:
Do nothing
when intervention is unlikely to generate meaningful incremental revenue.
8. AI Recovery Negotiator
An AI agent communicates with customers, understands their situation, negotiates payment terms, and adapts the conversation based on customer responses.
9. Dynamic Payment Plans
Generate merchant-approved installment plans and payment schedules based on customer context and recovery probability.

Maximum discount
Maximum installment duration

13. Real-Time Webhook Processing
Automatically update recovery workflows when payment events occur, with duplicate-event detection and idempotent processing.
14. Intelligent Customer Intervention Budget
Track how frequently a customer has been contacted and reduce or stop interventions when excessive communication is likely to hurt recovery or customer experience.
15. Human-in-the-Loop Escalation

User stories
Here are the final user stories for METIS, aligned with the finalized features and product flow.
METIS — Final User Stories
Merchant
US1 — View Revenue at Risk As a merchant, I want to see my failed and overdue payments and the total revenue at risk so that I know how much revenue needs attention.
US2 — View Customer Recovery Profile As a merchant, I want to view a customer's payment history, previous recovery attempts, and behavior so that I can understand their likelihood of recovery.
US3 — See Recommended Recovery Action As a merchant, I want METIS to recommend the best recovery action for each payment so that I don't have to manually decide how to recover it.
US4 — Understand the Recommendation As a merchant, I want to see why a particular recovery action was recommended so that I can trust the system's decision.
US5 — Compare Recovery Strategies As a merchant, I want to compare the expected outcomes of different recovery actions so that I can understand their potential financial impact.
US6 — Configure Recovery Policies As a merchant, I want to define limits for discounts, payment plans, contact frequency, negotiation attempts, and communication channels so that automated recovery stays within my business rules.
US7 — Simulate Recovery Policies As a merchant, I want to simulate different recovery strategies before deploying them so that I can estimate their expected financial impact.
US8 — Monitor Recovery Performance As a merchant, I want to monitor recovery rate, incremental revenue, intervention costs, and net recovery so that I can measure whether METIS is actually improving my revenue.

AI Recovery System
US9 — Identify At-Risk Payments As METIS, I want to identify failed or overdue payments that have a realistic chance of recovery so that recovery resources are focused on valuable opportunities.
US10 — Predict Natural Recovery As METIS, I want to estimate whether a customer will pay without intervention so that unnecessary recovery actions can be avoided.
US11 — Predict Intervention Outcomes As METIS, I want to estimate the expected outcome of every permitted recovery action so that I can compare possible strategies.
US12 — Estimate Incremental Recovery As METIS, I want to estimate how much additional revenue each intervention is expected to generate compared with doing nothing so that decisions are based on incremental value.
US13 — Optimize Recovery Value As METIS, I want to select the action with the highest expected incremental net revenue while considering costs, concessions, and customer fatigue so that recovery is economically optimized.
US14 — Decide Not to Intervene As METIS, I want to choose no action when an intervention is unlikely to create meaningful incremental revenue so that merchants do not waste recovery resources.
US15 — Execute Recovery Actions As METIS, I want to execute approved recovery actions through supported payment and communication channels so that recommendations can become actual outcomes.
US16 — Negotiate with Customers As METIS, I want to communicate with customers and negotiate approved payment terms within merchant-defined limits so that difficult payments can still be recovered.
US17 — Generate Payment Plans As METIS, I want to create suitable installment plans within merchant policies so that customers who cannot pay immediately have another path to repayment.
US18 — Enforce Guardrails As METIS, I want to validate every financial action against merchant policies before execution so that the AI cannot perform unauthorized actions.
US19 — Manage Customer Contact Frequency As METIS, I want to track previous interactions and customer fatigue so that customers are not repeatedly contacted unnecessarily.
US20 — Escalate Uncertain Cases As METIS, I want to escalate low-confidence or policy-sensitive cases to a human so that risky decisions are not fully automated.

Payment & Integration
US21 — Execute Through Razorpay As METIS, I want to use Razorpay APIs/MCP to create payment links and verify payment status so that recovery can happen through the payment infrastructure.
US22 — Process Payment Events As METIS, I want to receive and process Razorpay webhook events so that recovery workflows automatically update when payment status changes.
US23 — Prevent Duplicate Actions As METIS, I want to detect duplicate webhook events and maintain idempotency so that the same financial action is never executed twice.

Experimentation & Learning
US24 — Run Recovery Experiments As a merchant, I want to compare control and treatment groups so that I can determine which recovery strategies actually work.
US25 — Measure Incremental Lift As a merchant, I want to measure incremental recovery rather than just total recovery so that I know how much revenue METIS actually caused.
US26 — Learn from Outcomes As METIS, I want to use actual recovery outcomes to improve my prediction and optimization models so that recovery decisions become more effective over time.
US27 — Maintain an Audit Trail As a merchant, I want every prediction, decision, policy check, agent action, and payment outcome recorded so that recovery activity is fully traceable.

Customer
US28 — Receive Personalized Recovery Assistance As a customer, I want recovery communication to reflect my situation and payment history so that I receive relevant rather than generic reminders.
US29 — Negotiate Payment Terms As a customer, I want to explain why I cannot pay immediately and receive eligible payment options so that I can choose a realistic way to complete the payment.
US30 — Complete Payment Easily As a customer, I want to receive a secure payment link or payment-plan option so that I can complete the outstanding payment with minimal friction.

8. Decision & Optimization Engine
The optimizer chooses the best recovery action.
Technologies:
Google OR-Tools
Used when multiple constraints need to be optimized simultaneously.

Reminder     → ₹400
Payment Plan → ₹4,800

API design principles
REST for application APIs
Webhooks for asynchronous Razorpay events
Typed tool calls for the AI agent
Idempotency keys for financial operations
JWT/OAuth-style authentication for application access
Pydantic schemas for every request/response
Versioned APIs: /api/v1/...
Strict separation: LLM → policy engine → financial action
This is the API layer I'd use for the final METIS implementation.
